# Data Analyst Job Simulation — OneRoadmap.io

## 📊 Overview

Completed a **Data Analyst Job Simulation** through **OneRoadmap.io**, designed to replicate the type of analytical work performed by an entry-level data analyst in a real business environment.

The simulation involved working with a **5,000+ row sales dataset**, following business-defined data quality rules, analyzing revenue performance, calculating growth metrics, and communicating findings to an executive stakeholder.

Rather than focusing only on technical execution, the simulation emphasized the complete analyst workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Analysis → KPI Calculation → Business Insights → Recommendation**

---

## 🎯 Business Scenario

The simulation placed me in the role of a **Data Analyst** supporting a business stakeholder who needed reliable sales data and actionable insights for decision-making.

The work was divided into two analytical tickets:

### DA-101 — Clean the Client's Sales Export

A 5,000+ row sales export from a system migration contained data quality issues that needed to be resolved before analysis.

### DA-102 — Answer the COO's Three Questions

Using the cleaned dataset, the objective was to analyze revenue performance, growth trends, and channel contribution and provide a concise recommendation to the COO.

---

# 🧹 Ticket 1 — DA-101: Data Cleaning

### Objective

Prepare a messy sales export for analysis by applying the client's defined data-quality rules.

### Key Tasks

* Identified and removed duplicate records
* Standardized inconsistent date values
* Corrected data types
* Cleaned inconsistent categorical values
* Used lookups to validate and enrich records
* Identified data quality issues
* Applied systematic cleaning rules
* Validated the final dataset
* Prepared the cleaned dataset for downstream analysis

### Deliverable

**Cleaned sales dataset + data-quality note**

The objective was not simply to make the spreadsheet look clean, but to ensure that the resulting dataset was **reliable, consistent, and analysis-ready**.

---

# 📈 Ticket 2 — DA-102: Revenue & Growth Analysis

### Objective

Use the cleaned dataset to answer three business questions from the COO's perspective.

### Analysis Performed

* Calculated total revenue
* Analyzed monthly revenue trends
* Calculated month-over-month growth
* Analyzed revenue contribution by channel
* Calculated and evaluated Average Order Value (AOV)
* Compared channel performance
* Identified important revenue trends
* Evaluated whether the available data supported the proposed business decision

### Analytical Approach

The analysis focused on more than absolute revenue.

I considered:

**Revenue → Growth → Channel Share → AOV → Business Recommendation**

This helped distinguish between channels that generate the most revenue and channels that demonstrate stronger growth or customer-value characteristics.

# 🧠 What I Learned

This simulation strengthened my understanding of how data analysts work with imperfect business data and translate it into actionable information.

Key takeaways:

* Real-world datasets often require significant cleaning before analysis.
* Data quality directly affects the reliability of business decisions.
* Revenue alone is not enough to evaluate channel performance.
* Growth rate, channel share and AOV provide additional business context.
* Analytical recommendations should be supported by measurable evidence.
* Communicating insights clearly is as important as performing the analysis.
